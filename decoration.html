— FAQ —

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Our Decoration Services

What decoration packages do you offer?

▼
We offer customized packages for all types of events including weddings, engagements, receptions, birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, and corporate events. Each package can be tailored to your budget and preferences.

Do you provide customized themes?

▼
Yes! We specialize in creating unique, customized themes tailored to your vision and preferences. Our design team works with you from concept to execution to bring your dream decoration to life.

Do you travel outside Begusarai?

▼
Absolutely! We provide our decoration services across multiple cities including Barauni, Teghra, Bachhwara, Samastipur, Khagaria, and other parts of Bihar. Contact us for details about your location.

What is included in wedding decoration?

▼
Wedding decoration includes pandal setup, flower arrangements, stage decoration, lighting, entrance gates, and all thematic elements. The exact inclusions depend on your chosen package and preferences.

How early should I book?

▼
We recommend booking 2-3 months in advance for major events like weddings. However, we accommodate last-minute bookings based on availability. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements.
